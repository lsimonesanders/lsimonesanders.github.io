Case Studies

A quarter behind. Delivered on time.



The Situation

IBM's z/TPF product runs on Z mainframe systems used by some of the world's largest organizations including financial institutions, airlines, and hospitality. And system reliability isn't optional. Closing the skills gap for the engineers and architects who run that infrastructure wasn't a nice-to-have. It was critical.

That meant building a series of interactive online courses for enterprise clients — system architects, security leads, developers — coordinating across 15+ stakeholders consisting of content professionals, engineers, designers, executive leadership, and clients simultaneously. There was no production playbook. We were building it from scratch.


What I Did

I joined mid-production on the first course and immediately raised the quality ceiling by taking ownership of video editing, animation, graphic design, voiceover narration, and accessibility, and brand compliance, while also contributing to the process framework previously established by a colleague.

When that colleague retired mid-production on the third course, I assumed full ownership of the production process and retrospectives. Projected delivery without intervention: a full quarter late. By using feedback from the SMEs during the retrospectives to identify the root cause (uncapped review cycles with no clear definition of scope), I redesigned the process, aligned the team, secured stakeholder buy-in, and delivered on time.


Impact

  • 3 courses project managed end-to-end
  • 300+ course participants engaged on IBM Training
  • 1 full quarter of schedule recovered through process redesign
  • Up to 15+ stakeholders coordinated per course
