No infrastructure? No problem.


The Situation

A 5-person nonprofit. 2 full-time staff. A first-of-its-kind national conference to build — with no infrastructure, no tools, no documentation, and no runway to slow down on existing work.




What I Did

Before the conference was even on the radar, I identified the organization had no project management system. I led the evaluation for Wrike, and individually implemented and trained my colleagues.

When the conference was greenlit, that foundation was ready. In roughly 9 months I built everything else from scratch:

  • Operations backbone: Wrike implementation, training, ongoing support
  • Conference infrastructure: registration, ticketing, payment processing, website, livestream
  • Vendor coordination: registration company, graphic design firm, social media contractors, livestream platform
  • Team leadership: managed a part-time employee; participated in hiring and trained two replacements to cover a planned absence, using documentation I created

All of this ran simultaneously, while maintaining normal nonprofit operations.


Impact

  • 400+ attendees at a first-of-its-kind virtual, national conference
  • Full operational infrastructure built in ~9 months, while maintaining normal nonprofit operations
  • Handoff documentation became ongoing organizational reference material

Tools

Wrike • Zapier • Stripe • Mailchimp • Sendinblue • Squarespace • Weebly • Brushfire • Facebook • Instagram
